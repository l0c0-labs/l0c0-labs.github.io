L0C0 (LOCO) is an independent cybersecurity research group exploring security across software, hardware, and systems.

Our work includes vulnerability research, hardware and firmware analysis, attack trend analysis, security paper and CVE reviews, and security tool development.

We combine collaborative research with hands-on experimentation to deepen our understanding of security and share our findings and tools with the community.
