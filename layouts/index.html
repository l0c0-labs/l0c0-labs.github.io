03 / ABOUT L0c0

Driven by curiosity.
Grounded in research.

L0c0 stands for Laboratory of Cyber Operations. We are an independent cybersecurity research group focused on understanding technology through hands-on investigation.

Our work spans vulnerability research, reverse engineering, malware analysis, and security tool development. This site is where we share our findings and the process behind them.
