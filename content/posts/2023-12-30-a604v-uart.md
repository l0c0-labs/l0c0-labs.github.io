In this post, I will explain how to access the UART pins on an ipTIME router with an Arduino.

First, disassemble the router to locate the UART pins. 

![ipTIME A604V UART](https://iw.it.eu.org/assets/a604v/uart/photo_2023-09-19_06-01-19.jpg)

![ipTIME A604V UART](https://iw.it.eu.org/assets/a604v/uart/photo_2023-09-19_06-01-32.jpg)

![ipTIME A604V UART](https://iw.it.eu.org/assets/a604v/uart/photo_2023-09-19_06-01-36.jpg)

These pins seem to be the UART pins.

![ipTIME A604V UART](https://iw.it.eu.org/assets/a604v/uart/photo_2023-09-19_06-01-39.jpg)

Now, I need to identify the UART pinout for this device.

A UART header typically includes TX, RX, GND, and a power pin such as 3.3V.

These may be PCB pads rather than pins.

Let's identify TX, 3.3V, and GND first. 

![ipTIME A604V UART](https://iw.it.eu.org/assets/a604v/uart/photo_2023-09-19_06-03-05.jpg)

Since the measured voltage is 3.3V, this pin could be TX or 3.3V.

GND is the pin at the end of the row, connected to the black probe.

![ipTIME A604V UART](https://iw.it.eu.org/assets/a604v/uart/photo_2023-09-19_06-03-09.jpg)

I got 3.3V again, so this could be TX or 3.3V.

![ipTIME A604V UART](https://iw.it.eu.org/assets/a604v/uart/photo_2023-09-19_06-03-13.jpg)

I measured a low voltage, so this appears to be RX.

Here is the pinout I identified.

![ipTIME A604V UART](https://iw.it.eu.org/assets/a604v/uart/image.png)

Let's connect them to the Arduino Uno to distinguish TX from the 3.3V pin. 

![ipTIME A604V UART](https://iw.it.eu.org/assets/a604v/uart/image2.png)

Router RX → Arduino RX

Router TX → Arduino TX

Router GND → Arduino GND

I tried opening a serial connection through the Arduino.

![ipTIME A604V UART](https://iw.it.eu.org/assets/a604v/uart/image3.png)

![ipTIME A604V UART](https://iw.it.eu.org/assets/a604v/uart/image-weird.png)

However, garbled characters appeared. This suggested that I was receiving UART data, but the baud rate might not match the router’s.

So... what should I do now?

I need to try different baud rates until I find a match.

![ipTIME A604V UART](https://iw.it.eu.org/assets/a604v/uart/content-image-1540319830787.gif)

This table lists serial baud rates.

After a few more tries, I found the correct baud rate and successfully connected via UART.

![ipTIME A604V UART](https://iw.it.eu.org/assets/a604v/uart/image4.png)
