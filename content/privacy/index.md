L0C0 operates this research website. This notice explains the analytics used on the public site, the preference stored in your browser, and the role of our hosting provider.

## Website analytics

We use [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) to understand which articles are read and where visits come from. The analytics script sends page and visit information to our account at `l0c0.goatcounter.com`.

Depending on the service's tracking settings, statistics may include page paths and titles, referring websites, campaign tags, visit counts, browser and operating-system information, language, screen size, and approximate location derived from the IP address. We use these statistics to evaluate readership and improve the site.

GoatCounter's analytics do not use cookies, localStorage, or persistent cross-site identifiers. This does not mean that no network information is processed.

According to [GoatCounter's privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy), the service temporarily processes the site name, IP address, and User-Agent in memory for up to eight hours to distinguish repeat visits. It does not store the original IP address or full User-Agent in its analytics database.

GoatCounter describes its default statistics as aggregate counts. It also offers optional individual-pageview collection, which is disabled by default; the actual data collected depends on the account's settings.

The hosted service is operated by Martin Tournoij in Ireland and stores data on Hetzner servers in Finland and Germany. Its privacy policy explains its processing and deletion practices. We have not set a separate fixed retention period for aggregate readership statistics; they remain in the service until removed through its settings or account deletion.

We do not use this analytics integration for advertising or sell its readership statistics.

## Browser preferences

The site uses a localStorage entry named `l0c0-theme` to remember your chosen light or dark appearance. This preference stays in your browser and is not sent to GoatCounter by our theme code. You can remove it by clearing this site's browser storage.

## Hosting and external links

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. [GitHub's documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages) states that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. That hosting activity is separate from GoatCounter analytics and is covered by the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Some articles load images from external hosts. Your browser contacts those hosts to display the images, which can expose network information such as your IP address. Following links to GitHub, LinkedIn, or other websites also brings you under those services' own privacy practices.

## Your choices and questions

You can block the analytics script or requests to `gc.zgo.at` and `l0c0.goatcounter.com` with browser privacy tools. This does not prevent the articles from loading.

For privacy questions, contact L0C0 through our [LinkedIn page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/l0c0). Please do not post sensitive personal information in public GitHub issues.

We will update this notice when our website's data practices change.
